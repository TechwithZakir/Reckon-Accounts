# Tally-Style Accounting Reports on ERPNext: Ledgers, Day Books, Registers and Funds Flow

Accountants in Bangladesh who grew up on Tally expect ledgers, day books, registers and group summaries at their fingertips. **Reckon Accounts** by Reckon Technologies Ltd. adds Tally-style accounting reports and navigation on top of ERPNext, without creating a second accounting engine.

## Detailed features

### Ledger views

- General Ledger Custom with a Tally-style presentation, distinct from the standard ERPNext General Ledger
- Account and party ledger views with running balances
- Full ledger export to CSV with headings and filter context

### Books and registers

- Transaction books, registers and summaries for day-to-day review
- Group Summary with account-level view, or hierarchy rollup when a group is selected
- Exception views to spot unusual entries

### Funds flow

- Funds flow view driven by working-capital groups you choose

### Filters and dimensions

- Company, From Date and To Date as the only mandatory filters
- Optional account, party and group filters validated against company and currency
- Dimension filters with multiple selections and explicit blank values
- Voucher type and payment type filters that restrict balances as well as rows

### Control and reliability

- Permission checks on every report
- Pagination and bounded exports
- Reads posted ERPNext data only: no duplicate masters, no changes to ERPNext core, no direct GL posting

## Built on ERPNext

Reckon Accounts is a reusable Frappe app for Frappe/ERPNext 15 and 16. It reuses standard ERPNext reports where they exist and adds the Tally accounting views that ERPNext is missing, all grouped under one Reckon Accounts workspace.

## Who it is for

Accountants, finance teams and auditors who work in ERPNext but prefer Tally-style books, plus businesses migrating from Tally.
